# The unsupported span

**Repository** · Devil-s-Advocate-Architect-
**Audience** · People weighing a proposal against credible objections.
**Concept** · A bridge drawn on tracing paper stands for the proposal. A red overlay disputes one span. A scatterplot carries the unresolved risks into view. The bridge is an editorial metaphor for testing assumptions.

## Primary prompt

Wide 2:1 editorial screenprint for a decision-auditing studio. Two translucent sheets of tracing paper, one pale ivory plan with a bridge in side elevation, one vermilion counterargument marking a single unsupported span. At right, a graphite graph-paper risk scatterplot with six amber, orange and red ink dots and a crosshair. Tactile charcoal, muted cream, signal red. Swiss poster composition, overhead flat lay, crisp engraving, generous safe margins. No lettering, numbers, UI or logos.

## Alternate prompt

Create a flat drafting plate on warm paper. Draw a truss bridge in indigo ink and circle a questionable support in carmine pencil. Across the facing charcoal plate, enlarge six risk points on an orthogonal grid. Keep the bridge and high-impact corner clear at thumbnail size. Use no text.
